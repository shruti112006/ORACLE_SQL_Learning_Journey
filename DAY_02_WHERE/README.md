
WHERE CLAUSE IN ORACLE SQL

* Purpose :

 1. This clause is used to filter the records on the basis of one or more conditions as specified in the query.

 2. For the filtering purpose we use comparision operator such as =, != , > ,< >= along with some special comparision operators such as
                Between And , IN , Like operators which gives additional features.

3. For combining 2 or more conditions logical operators such as AND, OR are used.
